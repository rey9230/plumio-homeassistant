Plumio Hogar

Turn an ESP32 into a Bluetooth proxy so Home Assistant knows which room each of your Plumio tags is in.

ESP32 (classic)

ESP32-WROOM / “ESP32 DevKit”. The cheapest; fine for most rooms.

ESP32-S3

More memory: for a big room or a house full of Bluetooth devices.

ESP32 + presence radar

With an HLK-LD2410 on GPIO16/17: also knows someone is in the room, even sitting still.

How

  1. Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a USB data cable. Use Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Install, pick the port, and enter your Wi-Fi when asked.
  3. In Home Assistant: Settings → Devices & services → it appears as discovered. Add it and assign it to its room.
  4. Install Bermuda from HACS. Your Plumio tags get their room on their own.

Your browser can't talk to USB? Install it from the ESPHome dashboard instead: the configuration is in the Plumio integration's repository.
